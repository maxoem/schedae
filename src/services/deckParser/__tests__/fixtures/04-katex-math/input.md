# Card 1

## Front
What is the formula for the Taylor series expansion of \( f(x) \) around \( a \)?

## Back
The Taylor series expansion of an infinitely differentiable function \( f(x) \) at point \( a \) is:

$$f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x - a)^n$$

Where \( f^{(n)}(a) \) denotes the \( n \)-th derivative of \( f \) evaluated at \( a \).

## Notes
Special case for \( a = 0 \) is known as the Maclaurin series.

# Card 2

## Front
What defines an eigenvalue \( \lambda \) and eigenvector \( \mathbf{v} \) for an \( n \times n \) matrix \( A \)?

## Back
A non-zero vector \( \mathbf{v} \neq \mathbf{0} \) is an eigenvector corresponding to eigenvalue \( \lambda \) if:

$$A \mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}$$

Equivalently, the characteristic equation must be satisfied:

$$\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$$

## Notes
The set of all eigenvalues is called the spectrum of matrix \( A \).

Linear Algebra
